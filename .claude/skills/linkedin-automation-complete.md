## 📚 SECTION 1: ADVANCED SEARCH

### Boolean Search Mastery

**Operators:**
- `AND`: Both terms required
- `OR`: Either term works
- `NOT`: Exclude term
- `"quotes"`: Exact phrase
- `()`: Group terms

**Examples:**
```
"software engineer" OR "developer" AND (Google OR Meta) NOT recruiter

(CEO OR "chief executive") AND (startup OR "early stage") AND (AI OR "machine learning")

"product manager" AND "B2B SaaS" NOT "looking for"
```

**Pro Techniques:**
1. **Title search**: `title:(PM OR "product manager")`
2. **Current company**: Search + filter by "Current companies"
3. **Past company**: Use "Past companies" filter
4. **School**: Filter by specific universities
5. **Zip code proximity**: Location filter

### Sales Navigator (If Available)

**Unlock These Filters:**
- Seniority: C-level, VP, Director, Manager, Entry
- Function: Engineering, Product, Marketing, Sales
- Company headcount: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1000+
- Company revenue: < $1M, $1M-$10M, $10M-$50M, $50M+
- Years in position: < 1, 1-2, 3-5, 5-10, 10+
- Geography: Down to city/county level
- Intent signals: Posted on LinkedIn, Changed jobs, Mentioned in news

**Lead List Building:**
1. Create search with all filters
2. Save as "Lead List"

4. Export list (if available)

## 📚 SECTION 2: SMART OUTREACH

### Connection Request Framework

**BEFORE SENDING:**
1. ✅ Profile matches criteria (job title, company, location)
2. ✅ Not already connected
3. ✅ Not pending request
4. ✅ Account looks active (posted/engaged recently)
5. ✅ Have specific reason to connect

**MESSAGE STRUCTURE:**
```
Hi [FirstName],

[HOOK - Why them specifically]:
- "I saw your post about [topic]"
- "We both know [Mutual Connection]"
- "Fellow [University] alum"
- "Impressed by [specific achievement]"

[CONTEXT - Who you are]:
I'm [your role] at [your company], working on [brief description].

[VALUE - What's in it for them]:
I think we could [exchange ideas/collaborate/help each other] on [specific topic].

[CTA - Clear next step]:
Would love to connect!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**HOOKS THAT WORK:**
1. **Mutual connection**: "I see we both know Sarah - small world!"
2. **Recent content**: "Loved your post about remote work challenges"
3. **Shared background**: "Fellow Berkeley grad here!"
4. **Company news**: "Congrats on your Series B!"
5. **Specific skill**: "Your expertise in React would be valuable to connect with"
6. **Location**: "Noticed you're also in SF - would love to connect with local [role]"
7. **Event**: "See you're attending [Conference] - me too!"

### InMail Power Formula

**Subject Lines That Work:**
- "Quick question about [their company]"
- "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"
- "Loved your post on [topic]"
- "[Their company] + [Your company] = ?"
- "Fellow [identity] here"

**Body Structure:**
```
Hi [Name],

[Paragraph 1 - WHY THEM]:
I came across your profile while researching [topic] and was impressed by [specific thing].

[Paragraph 2 - CREDIBILITY]:
I'm [role] at [company]. We've helped companies like [similar company] achieve [result].

[Paragraph 3 - THE ASK]:
I'd love to get 15 minutes on your calendar to discuss [specific value].

[Paragraph 4 - MAKE IT EASY]:
Here's my calendar link: [URL]

Or just reply with your availability.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

P.S. [Additional hook or social proof]
```

**InMail Tips:**
- Keep it under 200 words
- Mobile-friendly formatting
- One clear CTA
- Use their language/terminology
- Reference specific pain points
- Offer value upfront
